RESEARCH

Research experience

Sep 2023 – Present

Graduate Researcher, DeCoDE Lab

Massachusetts Institute of Technology
Advisor: Prof. Faez Ahmed
  • Surrogates for simulation (BlendedNet, BlendedNet++). Co-developed RANS datasets of blended wing body aircraft with surface pressure and skin friction fields, compared neural surrogates on unseen shapes, and generated designs for target lift-to-drag ratios that were then checked with CFD.
  • Multiphysics (BlendedNet++ Structures, BlendedNetV2). Co-developed an automated workflow that applies predicted aerodynamic loads to shell finite element models, producing an aerostructural dataset and inverse design benchmark. With nTop, organized a design challenge on it for the ASME IDETC-CIE 2026 Student Hackathon. Now developing a multi-fidelity framework that couples aerodynamics, lattice structures, propulsion, and batteries.
  • Multi-fidelity learning (FIRE, AutoMF, FIRE BO). Co-developed methods that combine inexpensive and high-fidelity simulations: FIRE predicts performance with uncertainty using tabular foundation models, AutoMF weights an ensemble of field predictors, and FIRE BO chooses which simulation to run next.
  • Generative design for battery thermal management (paper). Developed a diffusion model that generates battery cell layouts with a lower peak cell temperature while keeping the layouts feasible, with collaborators at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

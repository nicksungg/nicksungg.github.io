BlendedNet

MIT DeCoDE Lab with MIT Lincoln Laboratory

A public aerodynamic dataset of 999 blended wing body aircraft, simulated with RANS at about nine flight conditions each (8,830 cases), and a neural model that predicts pressure and skin friction at every point on the aircraft surface.

In the demo: set the planform and the flight condition, build the aircraft, optimize its shape for a higher lift-to-drag ratio, and color the surface with the predicted pressure or skin friction.

BlendedNet++

MIT DeCoDE Lab with MIT Lincoln Laboratory

12,492 aircraft geometries, each with a RANS simulation that resolves pressure and skin friction over the surface. A benchmark of five surrogate models on shapes held out from training, and a diffusion model that generates aircraft for a requested lift-to-drag ratio. CFD on the generated designs matched the targets with R² above 0.99.

In the demo: choose a flight condition and a target lift-to-drag ratio, get four candidate aircraft from the diffusion model, then predict the surface pressure and skin friction of the one you pick.

BlendedNet++ Structures

MIT DeCoDE Lab with nTop and MIT Lincoln Laboratory

An aerostructural dataset and inverse design benchmark. Aerodynamic surface forces predicted by BlendedNet++ load a parametric shell finite element model: 13,989 analyzed runs on 1,369 planforms, linking shape, flight condition, and structural sizing to airframe mass, internal volume, and stress. Designs chosen with the surrogate are verified again by finite element analysis. With nTop, I organized a design challenge built on this dataset for the ASME IDETC-CIE 2026 Student Hackathon.

In the demo: set the planform and the structural layout, build the outer surface and internal structure with nTop, and explore them in 3D. The Pareto explorer finds low-mass designs for a mission.

FIRE: multi-fidelity regression without training

MIT DeCoDE Lab · with Rosen Ting-Ying Yu and Faez Ahmed

Combines many inexpensive simulations with a few high-fidelity ones using tabular foundation models, and predicts a value with its uncertainty without retraining for each problem. It ranked highest in accuracy and uncertainty quantification across 31 benchmark problems.

ASME IDETC-CIE 2026 Student Hackathon challenge

Organized with nTop · MIT DeCoDE Lab · August 13–23, 2026

With nTop, I organized the BlendedNet++ Structures problem for the student hackathon. Students developed AI-driven design optimization methods to find the aircraft planform and internal structure with the lowest mass, under constraints on aerodynamic performance, internal volume, and maximum stress. We released 13,720 finite element designs, a lift-to-drag surrogate, and a parameterized nTop model for it.
